This repository documents the practical setup and operation of a two-participant fNIRS hyperscanning experiment. It is intended to help experimenters prepare the equipment, coordinate participant sessions, collect synchronized recordings, and complete post-session checks consistently.

Both the fNIRS 3-Tasks Manual and the Rapport Annotation Pipeline were authored by Sinem Demirkan during her position as a Research Engineer working with Professor Justine Cassell at Articulabo, from January 2024 to August 2025.

These documents provide structured guidelines for experimental setup, data collection, rapport annotation, and post-collection processing, contributing to the documentation and reproducibility of the research workflows.

### Data Collection

Two datasets were collected as part of the experimental study:

**1. Adult Pilot Dataset (September–December 2024)**

A pilot dataset comprising **20 dyads** was collected at the **Inria Paris Research Centre, France**, by Sinem Demirkan, Cindy de Arajullo Silva, Hasan Onur Keles, Sophie Etling, Barokshana Baskaran, and Gabrielle Alimi (September–December 2024), with Reem al Najjar joining the data collection efforts in November 2024 (November–December 2024).

**2. School Dataset (April–July 2025)**

A second dataset comprising **13 dyads** was collected between April and July 2025 at **École élémentaire Boileau (17 rue Boileau, 75016 Paris, France)**, involving pairs of school-aged children.
