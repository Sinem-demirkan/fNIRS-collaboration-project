This repository documents the practical setup and operation of a two-participant fNIRS hyperscanning experiment. It is intended to help experimenters prepare the equipment, coordinate participant sessions, collect synchronized recordings, and complete post-session checks consistently.

Both the fNIRS 3-Tasks Manual and the Rapport Annotation Pipeline were authored by Sinem Demirkan during her appointment as a Research Engineer working with Professor Justine Cassell at Articulabo, from January 2024 to August 2025.

These documents provide structured guidelines for experimental setup, data collection, rapport annotation, and post-collection processing, contributing to the documentation and reproducibility of the research workflows.
